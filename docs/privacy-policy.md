# Mr. Python privacy policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Mr. Python is an Android app for learning to program in Python.

## What data we collect

The app itself collects no data and needs no account or sign-in. The free version shows ads
from Google AdMob, and their provider processes the data described under "Ads". After you buy
the full version, ads disappear and no advertising data is processed anymore.

The app:

- requires no account or sign-in,
- connects to the internet only to handle purchases in Google Play, to check which purchases
  belong to your Google account, and to load ads in the free version,
- contains no analytics tools of its own,
- never sends your learning progress to anyone.

## What is stored on your device

Your learning progress (finished lessons, XP, day streak, hearts, league tier, avatar) and your
settings (reminders, language) are stored only in your device's memory. If Android's system
backup is turned on, this data may be included in your private backup in your Google account,
which you manage yourself in your phone's settings.

You can delete the data at any time: with the "Reset progress" button in the profile, by
clearing the app's data in system settings, or by uninstalling the app.

## Ads (free version only)

Between lessons, the free version shows ads provided by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited).
For this purpose Google may process, among other things, your device's advertising ID, IP
address, approximate location derived from the IP address, device information, and data about
ad impressions and clicks.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland you will see a consent
dialog on first launch. You can agree to personalized ads or decline, in which case ads are
not tailored to you. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings
(Privacy → Ads).

Details: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Buying the full version turns ads off permanently.

## In-app purchases

The full version (one-time) and heart refills are bought through Google Play. Google handles
the entire payment: the app never sees or stores card details or any other payment data. From
Google it only learns what has been purchased, and stores that on the device. Google's
processing of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

## Permissions

- **Internet and Google Play billing** – for purchases and restoring them, and for loading ads
  in the free version.
- **Advertising ID** – used by Google AdMob in the free version.
- **Notifications** – only for lesson reminders. You can turn them off in the profile or in
  system settings.
- **Run at startup** – only to restore scheduled reminders after the device restarts.

## League

Rivals in the weekly league are simulated on the device. They are not real people, and your
score is not sent anywhere.

## Children

The app is intended for people aged 13 and over and is not directed at children.

We will announce changes to this policy by updating this document and the date at the top.
